Delivery

Shipping policy

Orders are prepared for dispatch within 1–2 business days. Delivery time depends on your destination and the carrier; tracking details are shared when an order is dispatched.

  • Merchandise subtotal over $120Complimentary shipping
  • Merchandise subtotal of $120 or less$18 shipping
  • Order processing1–2 business days

The free-shipping threshold is calculated from the merchandise subtotal before tax and discounts. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

02

A little more time

Returns policy

Eligible full-price items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items should be unused and returned in their original condition and packaging.

  1. Contact the store team with your order details to begin a return.
  2. Pack the item securely and include any original packaging.
  3. Once the return is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the original payment method.

Return shipping costs and refund timing depend on the return method and payment provider. This demo site does not process real payments or refunds.

03

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?

Tracking information is shared after your order has been dispatched. Allow up to two business days for processing before dispatch.

When is shipping complimentary?

Shipping is complimentary when your merchandise subtotal is over $120 before tax and discounts. Otherwise, shipping is $18.

Can I return an item?

Eligible full-price items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in their original condition.

Do I need an account to check out?

Yes. Create an account or sign in before continuing to checkout. Demo accounts are saved locally in the browser where they were created.

Does this site take real card payments?

No. The payment step is a demonstration only. Do not enter real payment details; the site does not process or save card information.
